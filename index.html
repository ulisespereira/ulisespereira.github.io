Ulises Pereira Obilinovic

I work at the intersection of applied mathematics and systems neuroscience, building data-constrained mechanistic models of neural population dynamics. My goal is to learn circuit-level theories directly from large-scale recordings in behaving mice constrained by anatomy. To do this at scale, I use and develop machine learning methods that fit mechanistic models — structured recurrent networks — to simultaneous recordings of thousands of neurons. I work closely with experimental collaborators to test these theories and refine the models in a tight theory–experiment loop.
