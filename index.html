I work at the intersection of applied mathematics and systems neuroscience, building data-constrained mechanistic models of neural population dynamics. I use and develop machine-learning methods that fit structured recurrent networks to recordings of thousands of neurons in behaving mice, learning circuit-level theories in a tight theory–experiment loop.
